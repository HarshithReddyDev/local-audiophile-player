# Presets

Origins: **Built-in** (read-only starting points authored for this
player), **Device** (catalog copies), **AutoEQ**, **Imported** (file
formats), **Manual** (your saves), **Community** (user-supplied imports),
**Tutorial**. Favorites are a flag, not a copy.

- 51 built-ins: general, music, content, and listening EQ plus tone,
  dynamics, spatial, and combined DSP presets — all conservative,
  documented, with preamp headroom included.
- Details show bands, preamp, active modules, and a response preview
  (tonal shape only, never the exact audible outcome).
- **A/B preview** toggles the preset against your previous sound without
  editing the stored preset; one tap restores your sound.
- Import/export: native JSON (lossless), GraphicEQ, Equalizer APO subset,
  CSV — with loss warnings, never silent.
