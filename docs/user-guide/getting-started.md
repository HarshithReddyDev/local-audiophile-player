# Getting Started

Twelve steps from zero to music:

1. **Add music** — Music → Add files, or drag & drop audio anywhere.
2. **Browse** — All, Albums, Artists, Genres, Recently Played, Favorites.
3. **Play** — hover a row and press Play.
4. **Now Playing** — tap the player bar for artwork, progress, queue, lyrics.
5. **Select a device** — Devices, or stay on Global.
6. **Load an EQ preset** — EQ & DSP → Presets → Use.
7. **Adjust** — small moves first; watch the headroom hint under Preamp.
8. **Save a profile** — Devices → save the sound to that device.
9. **AutoEQ** — if a measurement exists for your device.
10. **A/B compare** — judge changes at matched loudness.
11. **Playlists** — from any track row's favorite/queue actions.
12. **Cloud (optional)** — Settings → Sources.

The in-app **Guides** destination has the full illustrated version of every
topic below.
